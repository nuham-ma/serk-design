## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Overview

**Serk Design by Meron** is an Addis Ababa-based boutique fashion house and bespoke couture studio specializing in high-end Ethiopian cultural garments (የሀበሻ ቀሚስ), contemporary cultural fusion silhouettes, men's ceremonial attire, and ready-to-wear boutique rental collections under its **Serk Rental** banner.

The brand's identity centers on honoring centuries of Ethiopian handweaving craftsmanship (*Shema* / ሸማ and *Tilet* / ጥበብ) while innovating modern fashion silhouettes tailored for high-profile celebrations, weddings, *Melse*, graduations, and evening galas.

## 3. Visual Identity & Design System

### 🎨 Color Palette
- **Imperial / Tilet Gold**: `#C5A059` & `#D4AF37`  
  *Directly reflects the intricate metallic gold and bronze embroidery threads woven into traditional hems and necklines.*
- **Obsidian Onyx**: `#121214` & `#0B0C0E`  
  *Evokes the luxurious, dark background aesthetic seen on the Serk showroom feature wall and high-fashion editorial backdrops.*
- **Habesha Shema Ivory / Natural Silk**: `#FAF8F5` & `#F4EFE6`  
  *Represents authentic, unbleached, and handspun Ethiopian cotton fabric.*
- **Royal Velvet Accents (Crimson & Royal Navy)**: `#88222A` & `#1C2A44`  
  *Derived from the custom wedding kaftans and starburst evening gowns showcased in the boutique collection.*

### 🔤 Typography System
- **Ethiopic Display & Body**: `Noto Sans Ethiopic` & `Abyssinica SIL`  
  *Provides authentic, legible, and culturally dignified rendering of Geez script for the default Amharic interface.*
- **Latin Editorial Display**: `Cormorant Garamond` & `Playfair Display`  
  *Evokes Parisian and Milanese fashion magazine typography, elevating the brand's international haute couture appeal.*
- **Modern Sans UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`  
  *Clean, contemporary sans-serif engineered for ultra-crisp mobile screen legibility.*

   - Sweeping royal embroidered capes over modern tailored trousers.

4. **Men's Cultural Attire & Couple Sets (የወንዶችና የጥንዶች አልባሳት)**:
